== Description ==

Your Website’s Next Hire: An AI Concierge That Engages, Qualifies, and Books Instantly

Makase Pro is an AI-powered website plugin that instantly engages, qualifies, and books leads over text messages. The plugin integrates Makase's platform directly into your WordPress site and upgrades your website contact form or chatbot to improve lead conversion rates. 

**Key Features:**

* Engage Instantly: 24/7 AI concierge that greets every visitor, answers FAQs, and speaks in your brand voice so there are no more missed leads.
* Qualify Automatically: Smart logic identifies service type, location, and urgency, then routes high-intent leads to your CRM, email, and SMS.
* Book Instantly: AI-powered scheduling that syncs with Google Calendar, Calendly, or your CRM to turn qualified conversations into confirmed bookings.
* Integrate Seamlessly: One-line install compatible with all major builders and platforms like GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Google Tag Manager, and more.

**How It Works:**

1. Install Makase — Add the Makase API ID to your plugin settings.
2. Train Your Concierge — Teach it your services, FAQs, and brand tone.
3. Go Live Instantly — Makase starts engaging visitors 24/7 in your voice.
4. Qualify & Book Leads — It routes real customers directly to your email, SMS, CRM, and calendar.
5. Watch Your Business Grow — Trust Makase to build relationships and get more revenue.

Currently serving agencies and local service businesses across the United States. 

**Third-Party Service**

This plugin connects to Makase (makase.com/pro) to provide AI-powered lead generation and booking functionality. When website visitors click the quote widget and enter their phone number, that information is transmitted to Makase's servers to facilitate the booking process.

By installing and using this plugin, you acknowledge that:
* Customer phone numbers and booking requests are sent to Makase

No visitor data is transmitted without the visitor explicitly entering their information and clicking to request a quote.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the plugin
2. Go to Settings → Makase Quote
3. Enter your Makase API ID (get this from your Makase vendor dashboard)
4. Enable the floating widget option if desired
5. Save settings - widget now appears on your site

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a Makase account? =

Yes, you need an active Makase vendor account. Sign up at makase.com/pro to receive your API ID.

= What data is sent to Makase? =

Only data that website visitors explicitly submit through the widget (typically phone numbers and service requests). No passive tracking occurs.

= How does SMS work? =

Leads enter their mobile phone number in the Makase widget on your website. Customers receive a text message in seconds from the business that has installed Makase. Makase handles conversations 24/7 in 80+ languages while keeping you informed and in the loop of lead engagement, qualification and booking. Makase will work on SMS and WhatsApp. Watch a quick demo here.
